Strategy notes
Higher-timeframe (weekly, daily, 4H, 1H) support and resistance sets the bias. A liquidity sweep is price taking out a prior swing high or low (or equal highs/lows) and then reversing. The Order Block is the last opposite candle before the move that follows the sweep. A Fair Value Gap is the three-candle imbalance that move leaves behind. Entries are taken on 15m or 5m. Scalp, intraday or swing is chosen per trade from how the market reacts. No indicators are used.
